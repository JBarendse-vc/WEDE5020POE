Brief history of the organisation

Beesland Slaghuis is a local butchery situated in the centercentre of Jeffreys Bay. The business supplies high-quality meat and animal products. The origins of the business began in 1999 in Vryburg, Northwest. It officially reopened in March 2012 in Jeffreys Bay by owners, Pieter and Tertia Geyer .
