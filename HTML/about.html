Our Mission

Providing high-quality meat and animal products in Jefferys Bay. Examples of the products: Grade A steaks, wors, mince, biltong, and dry wors and other meat products. The business is committed to providing excellent customer service and ensuring that customers have a positive experience when shopping at the butchery.

Our Vision

To be acknowledged as a high-quality meat product provider and be a preferred local butchery in Jeffreys Bay. Our vision is to become the most trusted and sought-after butchery on the eastern cape, known for our exceptional quality and service.

Our Values

Our values include integrity, customer satisfaction, quality, and community engagement.

Our Team

Our team consists of experienced local professionals dedicated to providing the highest quality service and highest quality products.
